# 01 — Executive Review

## Board charter

Approve only what we would proudly put our reputation behind — not what merely compiles.

## Executive questions

| Question                                                  | Answer                        | Why                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Would users trust Auvora with significant digital assets? | **Not yet for GA**            | Money rails are honest previews; live settlement + pen-test incomplete       |
| Would financial professionals recommend it?               | **Not yet**                   | Legal URLs placeholders; ops SSO incomplete; compliance pack open            |
| Would experienced crypto users switch?                    | **Maybe for beta**            | Aether UX is distinctive; they will not switch without live Send/Swap/Bridge |
| Would beginners feel comfortable?                         | **Yes in preview**            | Calm copy, education, labeled simulators after Phase 9                       |
| Would this strengthen the Auvora brand?                   | **Yes if we launch honestly** | Brand is craft + trust; overselling would damage it                          |
| Would we proudly launch publicly today?                   | **No**                        | Exceptional bar not met for public GA                                        |

## Overall experience

Auvora’s identity is clear: Mist/Lagoon Aether, calm confirmations, educational AI, staging ops console. Craftsmanship is high for a preview product. **Public launch excellence requires live rails, legal surfaces, and operational SSO.**

## What the board fixed in this review

Residual trust theater on Swap / Bridge / Staking success when offline; privacy/support placeholder labeling.

## Verdict summary

**NO GO — public launch.**  
Proceed with closed-beta only under explicit “preview / not production settlement” messaging.
