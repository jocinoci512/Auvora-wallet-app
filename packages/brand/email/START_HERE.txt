EMAIL SETUP — STILL REQUIRED
The HTML files are templates, not installed email settings.
For manually written messages: insert Auvora_Email_Logo.png into your email
provider's signature editor and select it as the default for new messages and
replies. Add your real name, role and contact details.
For automated app emails: have your developer host the email PNG at a public
HTTPS asset URL or attach it using a matching Content-ID. Replace the relative
image src in Auvora_Email_Template.html with that URL or cid reference. Replace
bracketed placeholders, then install the template in your mail-sending service.
The signature HTML is a starting layout for providers that accept HTML.
Test a delivered message in the email clients you use, including dark mode.
Recipients may block remote images; the image includes readable alternative text.
No email account, app or live website was modified as part of this asset delivery.

DESIGN RULES
Deep lagoon #102D32; teal #087F75; mint #B9F4D7; background #F2F6F3.
Use the primary logo on white/light backgrounds and reverse on dark lagoon.
Keep space around the mark; do not stretch, add shadows, or recolor each letter.
Use the symbol for tiny placements, where the WALLET descriptor is too small.
The app master is square and opaque; preview corners are illustrative.
Android adaptive-icon layers still need platform-specific packaging.
All delivered SVG lettering is outlined and requires no installed font.

VALIDATION
Logo variants rendered and visually reviewed together. SVGs use vector paths.
PNG dimensions and SVG text removal checked. Email HTML has not been tested
through a live mail provider.
