APP NAME:
Auvora Wallet

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Non-custodial multi-chain wallet. Keys stay on your device.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Auvora Wallet is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet. Your recovery phrase, private keys, and mnemonic stay on this device. Auvora does not custody your funds and does not sign transactions on a server.

Use Auvora to:
• Create or import a self-custody wallet
• View a multi-chain portfolio (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain, Tron, and more as listed in the app)
• Prepare send and receive flows with on-device review
• Protect access with a device passcode and optional biometrics
• Connect to dApps with WalletConnect via Reown when this build is configured — you approve every session; nothing is auto-approved

Current release limitations (Version 1.0):
• Live on-chain broadcast is disabled. Send, swap, and similar actions stay on-device as previews until broadcast is audited and enabled.
• Receive funding addresses remain locked. QR, copy, and share for deposit addresses are off — do not send real funds to this release.
• Fiat on-ramp checkout is not live. Buy remains a preview until partner rails are enabled.
• NFT features are not included.

Account sign-in (email) is optional and separate from your wallet vault. Signing in never uploads your keys.
